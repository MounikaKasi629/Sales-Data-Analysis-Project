# Sales Data Analysis Project

## Project Overview
This project analyzes sales data using Python and Pandas to understand sales performance and identify useful business insights.

## Objectives
- Analyze sales performance across product categories and regions.
- Identify the top-performing sales representatives.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Analyze payment methods and monthly sales trends.
- Identify useful insights to support business decisions.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset
The dataset contains 1,000 sales records and 14 columns, including:
- Sale Date
- Sales Representative
- Region
- Sales Amount
- Quantity Sold
- Product Category
- Customer Type
- Payment Method
- Sales Channel

## Key Findings
- Clothing generated the highest total sales among product categories, with total sales of 1,313,474.36.
- Food generated the lowest total sales among product categories, with total sales of 1,201,773.54.
- David generated the highest total sales among sales representatives, with total sales of 1,141,737.36.
- Credit Card was the most frequently used payment method, with 345 transactions.
- September recorded the lowest total sales among the 12 months, with total sales of 367,837.60.
- New customers accounted for the highest total quantity sold, with 13,184 units.

## Conclusion
This project uses data analysis to understand sales performance across product categories, regions, sales representatives, and customer types. The findings can help businesses identify successful products, evaluate sales performance, and make informed business decisions.
